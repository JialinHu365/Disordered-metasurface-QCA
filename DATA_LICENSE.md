## Refractive-index data

The refractive-index values used as input for the calculations originate from two different sources.



The refractive-index data for silver were taken from previously published work and are reproduced in this repository solely to enable reproduction of the calculations presented in the associated publication. The corresponding source is cited in the manuscript and Supplementary Material and is also referenced below:



P. B. Johnson and R. W. Christy, “Optical Constants of the Noble Metals,” Phys. Rev. B, vol. 6, no. 12, pp. 4370–4379, Dec. 1972, doi: 10.1103/PhysRevB.6.4370.



These published refractive-index data constitute third-party material and are not covered by the CC BY 4.0 license applied to the research data generated in the present work. Their reuse remains subject to the copyright and licensing terms applicable to the original source.



The refractive-index data for Titania  were provided within the research group by Nicolas Jacquot, whose contribution is acknowledged in the associated publication. These data are included in the repository solely as input data required to reproduce the reported calculations.



These internally provided refractive-index data are not covered by the repository's CC BY 4.0 data license and should not be redistributed independently without permission from the relevant rights holder(s).
